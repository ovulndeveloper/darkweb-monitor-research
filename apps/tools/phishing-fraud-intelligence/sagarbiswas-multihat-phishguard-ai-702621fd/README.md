PhishGuard is a fast-paced web game that trains your instincts against email phishing. Powered by a Dual-AI Consensus Engine (OpenRouter & Groq), it generates and verifies highly realistic, unique phishing scenarios in real-time. Can you spot the scam before the 10-second timer runs out?
