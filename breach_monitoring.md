## Breach Monitoring Best Practices
1. **Regular Checking**: Check HaveIBeenPwned monthly for personal accounts
2. **Domain Monitoring**: Set up domain monitoring for organizational protection
3. **Credential Rotation**: Rotate passwords after breach notifications
4. **MFA Enablement**: Enable multi-factor authentication after breach discovery
5. **Credit Monitoring**: Consider credit freeze if financial data compromised
6. **Legal Compliance**: Follow breach notification laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
